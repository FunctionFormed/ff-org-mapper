About your data

Where is it saved?
Your data is saved to your browser's local storage — it stays on this device and is never sent to any server.
⚠️ Things to know
  • Clearing browser data — clearing cookies or cache will permanently delete your directory
  • Different browser or device — data doesn't sync; each browser keeps its own copy
  • Private or incognito mode — data is wiped when the window closes
  • Browser updates or reinstalls — can clear local storage
✓ Recommendation
Export regularly to download a backup to your computer — the easiest way to keep your data safe and move it to another device.
Your data & privacy
  • Device security — your data is protected as long as your device is locked when unattended
  • Browser extensions — risk is minimal with reputable extensions; we recommend periodically reviewing what you have installed
  • Exported backup files — treat any exported file as you would a standard work document; avoid storing in shared locations or sending via unencrypted channels
